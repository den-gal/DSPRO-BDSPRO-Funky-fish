FischerTechnik Learning Factory — Test Datasets
=================================================

CSV captures of NebulaStream MQTT sources reading the FischerTechnik
"Training Factory Industry 4.0 24V" broker (192.168.0.10:1883). Columns are
the same across all three datasets; only duration and content differ.
Timestamps are Unix epoch milliseconds. Header types follow
`name:TYPE:NULLABILITY`.

Files and columns
------------------

factory_bme680.csv — BME680 environmental sensor, HBW base plate. ~60s period.
  ts, t (temp °C), rt (raw temp, always 0), h (humidity %),
  rh (raw humidity, always 0), p (pressure hPa), iaq (air quality index),
  aq (IAQ accuracy 0-3), gr (gas resistance Ω, always 0)

factory_broadcast.csv — TXT controller identity/keep-alive. Fires on
connect then rarely; usually empty.
  ts, hardwareId, hardwareModel, softwareName, softwareVersion, message

factory_ldr.csv — photoresistor, wired to TXT. ~60s period.
  ts, br (derived brightness), ldr (raw resistance Ω)

factory_nfc_ds.csv — NFC reads at the DPS station, via PLC/Node-RED
(f/i/nfc/ds). No timestamp on this topic.
  workpiece_id (tag UID), workpiece_type (RED/WHITE/BLUE/NONE),
  workpiece_state (e.g. RAW, PROCESSED, NONE)

factory_nfc_ds_local.csv — same NFC reader, read directly off the TXT's
I²C bus (fl/i/nfc/ds).
  ts, workpiece_id, workpiece_type, workpiece_state

factory_order.csv — order-state tracking (f/i/order).
  ts, state (ORDERED/IN_PROCESS/SHIPPED/WAITING_FOR_ORDER/...),
  type (order color; empty when no order active)

factory_ptu_pos.csv — pan/tilt position of the SSC camera mount (i/ptu/pos).
  ts, pan, tilt (unitless, undocumented range)

factory_station_state.csv — per-station status (f/i/state/<station>).
  ts, station (hbw/vgr/mpo/sld/dsi/dso), code (status/error code),
  description (nullable, usually empty), active (bool),
  target (nullable, only set for vgr code=2)

factory_stock.csv — HBW's 9 storage slots (f/i/stock). Slot index 0-8
maps row-major to A1,A2,A3,B1,B2,B3,C1,C2,C3.
  ts, then per slot N: slotN_location, slotN_workpiece_id (tag UID, "0" if
  empty), slotN_workpiece_type (empty if slot empty), slotN_workpiece_state
  (empty if slot empty) — all three workpiece fields nullable


1. TwoOrdersComplete/
----------------------
Two orders (one RED, one WHITE workpiece) placed and shipped end-to-end.
factory_order.csv shows both going ORDERED -> IN_PROCESS -> SHIPPED, and
factory_stock.csv shows slots A3 and B2 emptying over the run.

Duration: 10 min 18 s (617.5 s), ts 1790771603215 to 1790772220720.


2. RawMaterialDelivery/
-------------------------
Two raw workpieces (BLUE, WHITE) scanned in at the DPS station and stocked
into the HBW. No order is placed. factory_order.csv and
factory_broadcast.csv are empty; factory_nfc_ds.csv shows the two scans
resolving to BLUE/RAW and WHITE/RAW.

Duration: 5 min 53 s (352.97 s), ts 1790772538962 to 1790772891932.


3. Idle/
---------
Factory idle, no orders and no workpieces moved. factory_broadcast.csv,
factory_nfc_ds.csv, factory_nfc_ds_local.csv and factory_order.csv are
empty; factory_stock.csv is identical in every row (no slot changes).

Duration: 3 min 29 s (208.77 s), ts 1790773331648 to 1790773540420.
